# VEILWARD — Art quality gate (not yet approved)

The user-provided three-quarter top-down snowy village screenshot is the **non-negotiable golden reference**. The reference defines art scale, texture density, layered cliffs, houses, water, lighting, character proportions, and atmosphere; it does not prescribe snowy weather everywhere.

## Visual requirements
- Rich 32-bit-style authored pixel tiles and sprite animations, no generic icon or geometric placeholder visuals in the approved prototype.
- Elevated 3/4 perspective, with believable roof facades, cliff height, shadows, occlusion, and bridges.
- Small expressive actors compared to buildings; animation for eight-way idle/walk, attack, dodge, hit, cast.
- Every biome equally polished: winter village, spring forest, cave interior.
- Soft cool shadows with animated warm lantern, hearth, window lights; highly textured snow/grass/wood/stone/water.
- Mobile landscape interface cannot obscure the view or offer unreadable text.

## Current truth
The v0.1 runtime has procedural original Canvas-drawn scenery and characters. It demonstrates scene composition and gameplay but is **NOT YET** a match for the creator's reference or a finished pixel-art asset pipeline. Don't represent the first canvas imagery as commissioned sprites or game-ready production art.

## Next tasks before prototype art sign-off
1. Create a consistent source pixel atlas, tile sizes and actor proportions.
2. Replace procedural building/tree/rock/actor shapes with authored pixel assets.
3. Add coherent materials, ambient shadows, roof occlusion and terrain transitions.
4. Capture runtime screenshots on real landscape iPhone, compare side by side against the reference.
5. Resolve all visible art clashes, seams, clipping and jarring zoom.
6. Only then progress to next quality gate of deeper combat, NPC routines and questing.

The full Hollowreach slice requires more than this first pass. The wider expansion roadmap must not be set until the creator has played and approved the polished slice.
